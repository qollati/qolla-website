A personal planner for iPhone

Qolla

Qolla is a place to keep track of your everyday tasks, commitments, plans and notes.

Some things need doing today. Others can wait. Some take a little longer to work through. Qolla gives you somewhere to keep all of them without having to plan everything at once.

Created with neurodivergent people in mind, Qolla offers a flexible approach to planning that anyone can use.

Designed for an imperfect day.
